What each is:
- frame.csv: the frame file, 538 rows (the 151 binary entries of stages 1 to 3, then the UCI census's 387 in draw
  order), the sha256 the dataset rule states.
- snapshot-sha256sums.txt: the checksums of the published records the frame was built from (the suite and
  curation snapshots, the UCI listing and its draw order, and the scripts that built the frame), among them the
  curation record's and the draw order's sha256 the dataset rule states.
- second-screen-passers.txt: the second screen of the seven passers that had rested on one screener (rule read at
  ed9e2b4e8).
- second-screen-census.txt: the second screen of the census entries in draw order, to Mammographic Mass (rule read
  at e2aee00c9, its continuation at a7984db18). The readings the rule took of it are in the dataset rule's
  section 5.
- referee-rule.txt: the referee of the first dataset rule (plan at 59a3dc165).
- referee-guard-round1.txt, referee-guard-round2.txt: the referee of the real-data guard and the redesigned rule,
  rounds 1 and 2 (7719f8793, ed9e2b4e8).
- referee-sizing-round1.txt, referee-sizing-round2.txt: the referee of the sizing of R2 and R3, rounds 1 and 2
  (a7984db18, 36c75a02e). In round 1 seven non-ASCII symbols are spelled in ASCII (theta, beta, ~, +/-, -).
