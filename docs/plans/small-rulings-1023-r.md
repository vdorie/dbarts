## Steps

1. predict-training-weights. predict with no newdata takes weights: they replace the fit's own for the training rows' posterior predictive draws, one value or one per training row, from the stored draws with no trees, acting as at newdata for each family and type (R/generics.R trainingRowWeights, refuseUnfitPredictiveWeights, shared with the newdata path). Test: test-predict-training-rows.R, equal to the call at newdata = the training data with the same weights for gaussian, Student-t, heteroscedastic, aft, probit and logistic, a fit without trees, the fit's own weights given back, the refusals.
2. predict-training-bases. predict with no newdata takes bases: they replace the fit's own at the training rows, the stored draws moved by the change in each forest's (basis %*% glue) * forestFits term, with no trees (R/generics.R withTrainingBases). An offset beside them applies as well. A fit without forestFits or training draws is refused naming the fix. Test: test-predict-blend.R, the oracle (the call at newdata = the training data, to rounding) on a forest of levels and a forest of numbers, every type, split and combined chains, binary fits, a fit without trees, the fit's own bases bit for bit, the refusals.
3. ppd-weight-zero-na. On a gaussian, Student-t or aft fit a row at weight 0 has NA posterior predictive draws by every route (extract, fitted, the test sample, predict at newdata, predict with no newdata), with no warning: sampleFromPPD draws the row at weight 1 and sets its draws to NA. Test: test-predict-training-rows.R, the rows kept identical to the draws of the build before, ci.level, the test sample, Student-t, aft, heteroscedastic, and the binary fits unchanged. Help in bart.Rd and bartBT.Rd, NEWS.
4. hazard-one-class-fit. A hazard fit whose person-period response holds one class is fitted with one warning worded for subjects and events (R/spec.R warnSingleClass), at bart and dbarts, x/y, matrix response and formula. Test: test-family.R.
5. survival-sample-argument. survivalProbabilities with no newdata is always the training subjects; the stored test subjects are asked for with sample = c("train", "test"), on the hazard family (no trees) and the aft family (stored test fit and, heteroscedastic, test scale; no trees). sample = "test" with newdata, or on a fit with no test set, is refused. An offset with no newdata replaces the offset of the subjects asked for. Test: test-hazard.R, test-aft.R, test-aft-heteroscedastic.R, test-row-names.R.
6. forest-names-by-label. Every per-forest margin of a fit (fit$fixed$k and the held amplitude, names(fit$leaf.prior), extract(type = "k"), summary's fixed k, forestFits and varcount, glue's forest key, and so extract, fitted and predict with type = "forest") is named by the forest's label, forest<i> for a forest with none; forest<i> still selects position i (R/bart.R forestMarginNames). Test: test-forest-labels.R, and the tests that read a labeled forest by forest<i> restated.
7. perturb-cuts-cap-refused. n.perturb.cuts takes a whole number from 1 to 65533 or Inf and refuses a finite value above the cap in dbartsControl, at validation, at creation and at setControl, in n.cuts's wording (R/A_class.R perturbCutsRefusal). Test: test-perturb-width.R.

## Calls made in the build

- Item 1: replacement weights are checked for form on every type, a numeric vector of one value or one per training row with none missing (the offset's rule, worded alike), where at newdata only a posterior predictive draw looks at their values and na.action answers a missing one; the family's check (finite and non-negative precision multipliers, positive integer trial counts) runs for type "ppd" alone, as at newdata. The alternative is the newdata path's looser acceptance (a character weight taken with type "ev"); this reading can be loosened later and not the other way. A one-column matrix is refused. type = "forest" checks the form and ignores the value; type = "sigma" keeps its refusal of weights.
- Item 2: the draws are moved by the change in each forest's term rather than rebuilt from the response shift, because the shift lives in the sampler, which a fit without trees does not keep; the result equals a rebuild to rounding (about 4e-15 against the call at newdata), and a basis equal to the fit's own moves nothing, bit for bit. A list entry of NULL for a forest that carries a basis is refused, as at newdata, worded for the training rows; the alternative is that NULL keeps the fit's own basis for that forest, a convenience a caller can write by passing fit$bases. A fit kept without its training draws is refused in extract's words; one kept without forestFits in the words of the combined arms.
- Item 3: the brief says ppd at weight 0 was NA with R's "NAs produced" in 0.9-34; dec-B372's text says the branch before it gave that and that 0.9-34 used sigma at every row (0.358 at every weight). The NEWS entry keeps what it already said of 0.9-34 (sigma at every row) and states the NA. A length-one weights vector is expanded to the rows before the held rows are found. A credible band over a row with no draws is NA in all three columns (posteriorInterval), where quantile would have refused it.
- Item 3, the other families at weight 0 on these routes, found and left as they are: probit, ordinal and nbinom take 0/1 weights as the active-row mask and draw a masked row's posterior predictive from its latent, finite, at extract and fitted; predict at newdata refuses weights on ordinal and nbinom by name (no weight in the draw) and refuses 0 on probit and logistic as a trial count that must be a positive integer; logistic cannot be fitted at weight 0; multinomial takes no weights and draws a zero-trial row from its probabilities; hurdle takes no weights; a hazard fit's weights follow its binary link's policy, so a masked subject's rows draw finite values and a logistic hazard cannot hold weight 0. rbart's posterior predictive takes no weights.
- Item 4: the warning says "no event: every subject is censored" or "no period at risk without an event: every subject has its event in the first period", then which way the prior drives the hazards and that the survival curves sit where the prior puts them. refuseNonBinaryResponse lost its hazard branch and its unused arguments, and refusesSingleClass is now fitsSingleClass. xbart does not fit hazard responses, so it has no hazard door. The hazard fit's weights policy is as before.
- Item 5: sample is the last formal of the method, after na.action, so no positional call moves (survivalProbabilities(fit, times, newdata, offset, combineChains) is as it was); the alternative is after offset, as in extract. An explicit sample = "train" beside newdata is taken and ignored; the alternative refuses it with "test", which would also refuse a loop over both samples. A hazard fit kept without trees and with a test set names sample = "test" in the bare call's refusal. The aft test path is extract(sample = "test") on the fit with its test offset replaced (withTestOffset, from offset.test), so a heteroscedastic fit reads s.test, refused naming keepFits where it is missing. The reasons table for names foreign to the method lost sample.
- Item 6: the names come from the labels the sampler records at creation, forest<i> where a forest has none and for a multinomial fit's category forests, which have no labels. The trees data frame's forest column is a position number and is unchanged. The sampler's own readers ($getForestFits, $getK) carry no forest names and are unchanged; $getLeafPrior was already named by label. bartCause reads forestFits and glue by position and does not read these names; stan4bart and treatSens do not read them.
- Item 7: the cap is mirrored in R (maxCutsRepresentable, 65533), pinned beside the bridge's own refusal of n.cuts above it by test-engine-constants.R and test-perturb-width.R. setControl runs the one check and not validObject, which would add refusals the item does not name. The bridge's clamp is untouched, reached by Inf and by a slot written past every R check.
