# Decision register

Every design decision the bartcore branch carries onto main, merged from the five code-side slices, the diff-only review and the two evidence sweeps; one row per choice, with the slice ids it merges listed in `sources`.

Attribution rule: a decision is VD's only if the record quotes VD choosing, or lays out alternatives and records VD's pick (evidence class `quote` or `choice`). "VD approved", "per VD", "proceed at your discretion", a date-stamped marker naming VD with no fork put to VD, or nothing at all is tacit permission and counts as agent-made (`tacit` or `none`).

How to mark a row: write `mine`, `not mine` or `revisit` in the VD column. Section A is the set that needs adjudication; section B is for a confirming scan; section C expects no action.

Citation rule: any later document, plan or commit that would say "VD decided" cites a row id here instead. A claim with no row here has no attribution.

Sections: A, agent-made with a cost, sorted by cost; B, decisions with maintainer evidence; C, agent-made with no identified cost.

Columns: `fixes` is what the choice settles; `forecloses or costs` is what it gives up; `axis` is feature, UX, perf, maint or convention; `evidence` is the class plus the verbatim quote when the class is quote or choice; `record` is the path making the attribution claim; `sources` is the slice ids merged.
